## Full description (≤ 4000)

🌱 → 🌿 → 🌳  Every match is an evolution.

EMOJIVERSE is a match‑3 saga where the pieces are the emojis you use every day. Line up three identical emojis and they don't just vanish — they **evolve** into the next one. Match four for a 🚀 rocket, five for a 🌟 wild star, or an L‑shape for a 💣 bomb. Swap two specials together for screen‑shaking combos.

🗺️ **7 emotional worlds, 140 hand‑tuned levels**
Joy, Melancholy, Fury, Dream, Fear, Love and Wonder — each world introduces one new mechanic (rocks, ice, dust, cages, odd boards…) with a boss at the end.

🧪 **The Laboratory**
Pieces you evolve on the board go to your lab. Mix two ingredients in the cauldron: 😢 + 😡 = 🥺, 💧 + 🔥 = 💨, 🐛 + 🌿 = 🦋. 71 recipes to find — a wrong mix never costs anything and always gives you a hint.

📖 **The Emoji‑dex**
144 emojis to collect across 12 families, with rarity tiers, silhouettes and “echo” hints for the ones you haven't found.

🎭 **Weekly vault events**
Masquerade, Fiesta, Legends and Secrets rotate every week and unlock 48 legendary vault emojis — 🐉 🦄 🔮 ♾️ and more.

✨ **Made to feel good**
• Living emojis that breathe, squish and stretch
• Procedural music that changes with every world
• Generous power‑ups, no timers, no pressure
• Works offline, tiny download, no ads

Free to play, made with Google's Noto Emoji.
