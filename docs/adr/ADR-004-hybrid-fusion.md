# ADR‑004 — Hybrid fusion: evolve on the board, discover in the lab

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026‑09‑05

## Context
Pure cross‑recipes (A+B=C) inside a turn‑limited board make experimentation punishing; pure identical‑merge (Suika) lacks the surprise/discovery hook.

## Decision
Board = match‑3 of identicals → next tier (plus Candy‑Crush specials and synergies). Laboratory = cross recipes without turn pressure; discoveries unlock pieces that then appear on boards.

## Consequences
Both verbs feed the Emoji‑dex; the rules engine models only the board, recipes are pure content lookups.
